# Scientific claim-to-evidence matrix

All paths below are relative to `publications/paper1_review/` unless an absolute scientific-source path is given. This is a supplementary audit, so internal identifiers appear here but not as the paper's scientific vocabulary. The numerical tables are read-only derivations of finalized evidence. No inference was performed for this manuscript.

## Evidence levels

- **Analytical:** exact under the stated mathematical assumptions; not a measured network result.
- **Measured, conditional:** saved outcomes on a frozen checkpoint, executable, cohort, mask and fault law.
- **Exploratory:** observed comparison chosen or designed after related outcomes; not prospective confirmation.
- **Unsupported:** not claimed; requires additional evidence.

## Claims deliberately excluded

The manuscript does not claim radiation qualification, physical mitigation implementation, ASIC costs, superiority over PrioriFI without a measured allocation, full-model FP32/BF16 results from restricted pilots, causal proof of network redundancy, universal failure of static rankings, a whole-network conditional optimum, or new Boolean-function foundations. It also does not claim that unresolved differences are statistical equivalence.

## Remaining author decisions

Approve coauthor contributions/corresponding author, venue fit, final exposure language, and the deployment significance of severe-corruption/capped-loss regimes. Those judgments cannot be replaced by checksums or by a larger number of fault fields.
