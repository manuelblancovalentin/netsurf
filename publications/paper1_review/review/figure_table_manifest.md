## Verification scope

Every plotted condition mean was reconstructed from saved field-level observations and checked against the source summary. Source finalization identities, pairing metadata, event counts, clean/full equality, and the files used in figures were audited. The manuscript's curve build links omitted large original mask/event/prediction payloads by their source manifest checksums; it does not claim that every omitted byte was rehashed during this build. The historical interaction audit has its own stronger, explicit 112-bundle rehash receipt. The original low-BER or mismatched-clean Pythia study is not substituted for the newly clean-gated wide-BER source.

The main grid contains 1,254 condition labels and 35,136 saved policy/field observations over 1,248 separately scheduled fields. Among them, 1,344 FKeras labels duplicate MSB outcomes at complete tiers. Each W8A8 study has 4,800 actual faulted requests and 192 reused clean/full-control observations. These are accounting facts, not additional replication levels.

## Visual conventions

Current Figure 1 retains the original three-stage workflow's visual structure in editable TikZ with software-only labels. The delta-matrix/encoded-XOR drawing is no longer a main figure. The supplied historical PNG identities and reuse decisions remain in `review/legacy_figure_reuse.json` and `review/legacy_figure_reuse.md`; their previous-delivery status does not turn them into current measured evidence. No old numerical curve is imported.

Colors, means, and original interval endpoints are preserved in the compact display. Classification BER panels use a disclosed asinh vertical transformation so that near-zero beneficial changes and severe degradation remain visible; tick labels stay in original task units. ToyCar uses AUC-risk change. Transformers use capped NLL change. Curves connect measured points only. Means, uncertainty bands, and paired intervals are distinct from empirical outcome distributions. Current Figure 4 shows all 32 pool effects in raw nats/clip with deterministic vertical dodging and a separate mean interval; horizontal positions are never jittered.
