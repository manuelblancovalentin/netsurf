## Changes responding to the review

1. **Distinct methods, sometimes identical masks.** Figure 2 has separate MSB-first and published FKeras legend entries. Black and purple-dashed curves overlap exactly on the three TFLite panels at 12.5% coverage. Eight equally eligible bits per word imply that one eighth of the population contains every sign bit; a strict significance-first order selects the same set regardless of its within-tier ordering. At 25% both orders select two whole tiers. The 6.25% and 18.75% comparisons in Figure 3 can distinguish them. No curve is artificially offset and the identical uncertainty band is shaded only once. Transformer FKeras remains unmeasured.
2. **Table 3 coverage.** The original candidate study saved six score families, not FKeras. An offline audit now joins the later saved ResNet-8 Hessian scores to the same 320 candidate identities, frozen checkpoint, CIFAR archive, calibration indices and preprocessing. Its tier-first Spearman correlation is 0.286522, with 95% candidate-bootstrap interval [0.205905, 0.369940]. Raw absolute projection gives 0.273918, interval [0.189311, 0.355922], but ignores the bit tiers and is not substituted for the allocation rule. Natural score ties are retained. The main table contains only the tier-first value; the appendix labels the raw diagnostic separately.
3. **Two unavailable correlations remain visible.** The older KWS and ToyCar candidate records bind labels and indices but not the actual preprocessed calibration inputs. Their aggregate asset-lock digests differ from the later FKeras runs, and the complete historical locks/input digests needed to resolve the join are unavailable. Matching model identity, tensor mapping, preprocessing name and sample indices alone is insufficient. Their table cells remain dashes. This is a cross-run score–damage joining limitation, not a failure of their separately finalized FKeras protection evidence. No new inference or reconstructed calibration inputs were used.
4. **NetSuRF's role.** Its name appears once in explanatory prose as the software implementation of the evaluation workflow, not as a novel ranking algorithm, title brand or superiority claim. The workflow, exact-bit contract and reproducibility remain part of the paper.
5. **Useful expansion, not stronger unsupported conclusions.** Added explanations distinguish isolated-score association, mask protection quality and transfer across conditions; explain first-order aggregation, paired uncertainty and within-model preference reversals; and distinguish a changed conditional choice from established held-out benefit. The unresolved conditional-versus-isolated result remains unresolved. No result is attributed to interactions merely because methods differ.
