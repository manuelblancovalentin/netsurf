# Prospective stratified multi-pool language-model study: design gate

This is a **prospective design**, not a frozen executable campaign or effectiveness result. It follows the finalized 96-bit targeted pilots and the [representation audit](TRANSFORMER_LM_REPRESENTATION_AUDIT.md). Pythia FP32-stored/FP32-compute is the first feasibility target. Native-BF16-compute Pythia remains validation failed. The BF16-stored/FP32-compute Pythia path is a separate, paired storage-to-computation configuration; Qwen BF16/BF16 follows only after an independently costed pilot. No M11 or historical finalized evidence is modified.

## Sampling and estimand

The frame is all 48 actual Pythia decoder-layer dense matrices: four named classes in each of layers 0–11 (`attention.query_key_value`, `attention.dense`, `mlp.dense_h_to_4h`, `mlp.dense_4h_to_h`). Embeddings, norms, biases, buffers and output head are excluded and remain clean by design; they are not counted as protected. Safetensors metadata in the [stratified scaling design](TRANSFORMER_LM_STRATIFIED_SCALING_PROTOCOL.md) gives the 84,934,656-word frame. Before any fault outcome, enumerate and hash every stratum's tensor name, shape, ordinal range and checkpoint file. Report any tied or aliased storage explicitly.

Draw **eight independent candidate pools**, with reserved sampling seeds `981001–981008`. In each pool, draw one scalar word uniformly from each of the 48 tensor strata. On each selected word draw one sign bit (the only sign position), one exponent bit uniformly from the eight FP32 exponent positions, and one significand bit uniformly from the 23 FP32 significand positions. A pool has 48 words and 144 distinct candidate bits. A particular bit in class `c` of a tensor containing `N_t` words has within-pool inclusion probability `1/(N_t m_c)`, with `m_sign=1`, `m_exponent=8`, `m_significand=23`. Pools are sampled independently; repeated words between pools are allowed, recorded and accounted for in interpretation. The actual 144-bit panel, not the full model, is the Bernoulli fault population for this first multi-pool gate. The all-layer frame is a sampling frame, **not** an experimentally protected full decoder.

The principal risk is teacher-forced WikiText-2 target-token mean NLL with the existing 30-nat nonfinite/failure penalty and a separate failed-token fraction. Perplexity is a monotone secondary display. Pinned tokenizer, no BOS/EOS insertion, 1,024-token context, 512 stride and token-ID hashes must match the READY configuration. A proposed fresh calibration slice is validation token IDs **161–288**, scoring 162–288 (127 targets). A proposed shared final slice is test IDs **514–770**, scoring 515–770 (256 targets). The previous pilot scored test targets 1–256, and the separate exponent control has frozen targets 258–513. The new slices must pass a fresh exposure audit and be bound to the final manifest before any model-selection fault inference. Shared final tokens across pools and BER arms are deliberately paired and are never counted as independent replicates.

## Policies, faults and statistical family

Compare fixed-budget random, sign-first, the data-free canonical exponent-first control, and task-matched exact isolated-bit allocation. At budgets 18 and 36 of 144 bits, all policies have the same panel cardinality. Include clean, unprotected and full-panel logical-suppression controls. Isolated scores use the calibration slice only and retain signed harmful/beneficial effects. Exponent-first uses encoded class alone with canonical identity ties, as implemented in `netsurf/transformer_lm_representation_audit.py`; it is not tuned on final outcomes. All methods share each pool's final master fault field and token cohort. The initial planning BERs are `1/144` and `4/144`, for unprotected panel exposure λ=1 and 4. At budget 18, expected applied events are 0.875 and 3.5; at budget 36 they are 0.75 and 3.0. Record proposed, applied and suppressed events and exact stored-byte restoration. These panel probabilities must not be applied to the full multi-billion-bit model.

Reserve **32 independent final fields per pool per BER**, with disjoint selection and final seeds. All eight pools and both BER arms form one frozen family before any final field. A primary two-sided family can test exponent-first minus exact isolated at BER 4/144 for budgets 18 and 36, Holm-adjusted across two; the primary sign is fixed so positive favors isolated. The lower BER and random/sign-first contrasts require a separate prespecified secondary family and Holm adjustment before final execution. Report each pool's paired held-out mean, failure fraction and mask identities; then summarize eight pool-level effects with pool-level uncertainty. Field uncertainty is nested within pools, and shared final tokens create dependence across pools. Use a hierarchical pool/field bootstrap as a sensitivity analysis, retaining a conservative pool-level interval and a paired field-level table. Do not count 256 tokens, overlapping windows, or 8×32 fields as independent pool replicates. Report pool overlap, selection ties, selection instability, heavy-tail field diagnostics and whether sign/exponent effects depend on tensor class or layer. No pool may be dropped for an adverse outcome.

## Feasibility gate before freeze

With one clean plus 144 isolated calibration requests per pool, eight pools require **1,160 selection requests and 147,320 scored-target requests**. Ten final policy arms (four methods × two budgets, unprotected and full) over eight pools × two BERs × 32 fields require **5,120 final requests and 1,310,720 scored-target requests**. Total conservative cost is **6,280 requests and 1,458,040 scored-target requests**, excluding asset validation, repeated load and any deliberately nonreused duplicate mask. The prior Pythia FP32 targeted pilot recorded about 359 seconds from its first isolated response to selection seal for 96 isolated requests, and about 2,248 seconds from seal to analysis for 513 final requests while other campaigns shared the host. A linear extrapolation is roughly **7.5 hours**, not a throughput guarantee. Provision **12 foreground hours and at least 4 GiB RAM** for Pythia FP32 initially; measure one bounded, previously exposed-input tensor-stratum trial and a fresh cost pilot before freezing exact run resources. The full plan must fail closed on partial fields and seal all pool selections before final evaluation.

Qwen's analogous 28-layer × seven-class frame would produce 196 words/588 bits per pool at one word per stratum. Its sampling, budgets, BER, field count and token cost require a separate bounded gate; copying Pythia's panel BER or 12-hour envelope is not justified. Report inclusion probabilities and expected applied events for that separately frozen panel. Neither design supports whole-model optimal protection or physical fault-rate claims.
